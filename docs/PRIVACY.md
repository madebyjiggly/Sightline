# Privacy Policy — Sightline

_Last updated: 7 September 2026_

Sightline ("the app", "we") helps you track your spending against budgets. This
policy explains what data the app handles and how. **Fill in the contact email
and your legal/entity name before publishing.**

## What we handle

- **Account details.** When you create an account, your email and a password are
  sent to the Sightline server. Passwords are stored only as a salted **bcrypt hash**
  — never in plain text. A session token is stored securely in your device Keychain.
- **Budgets, goals & preferences.** Stored **on your device** (SwiftData / app
  settings). They are not uploaded unless you are signed in and syncing.
- **Bank data (only if you connect a bank).** With your consent, we access your
  account balances and transactions **read-only** through **Basiq**, Australia's
  accredited open-banking (CDR) provider. You log in on your bank's own page — we
  never see or store your bank credentials. This data is used solely to show you
  your spending inside the app.

## What we do NOT do

- No advertising, no third-party analytics, no cross-app tracking.
- We do not sell or rent your personal information.
- We never store your bank login credentials.

## Service providers

- **Basiq** — bank data aggregation under the Australian Consumer Data Right.
  See Basiq's privacy policy at <https://basiq.io/privacy-policy/>.

## Data retention & deletion

- Signing out removes the session from your device.
- To delete your account and any server-stored data, contact us at the email below
  and we will remove it.

## Children

Sightline is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect
their data.
