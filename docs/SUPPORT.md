## Common questions

**Is my bank login safe?**
Yes. You sign in on your bank's own page through Basiq (Australia's accredited
open-banking provider). Sightline never sees or stores your bank password.

**Do I have to connect a bank?**
No. The app works on sample data. Connecting a bank is optional and adds your live
balances and transactions.

**How do budgets and categories work?**
Set a monthly dollar budget per category. Add your own categories with match
keywords, and live transactions are sorted into them automatically.

**How do I delete my account or data?**
Email the address above and we'll remove your account and any server-stored data.

**Notifications?**
Sightline can alert you when a category goes over budget. Enable them from the bell
icon on the Home screen (all on-device — no external servers).
